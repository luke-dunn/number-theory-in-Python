Connection to P vs NP Problem

You then noted that finding a shortcut in factorization is connected to the P vs NP problem. I explained the P vs NP problem, where P represents problems that can be solved quickly (in polynomial time), and NP represents problems where a solution can be verified quickly. The factorization problem is believed to be in NP but not in P, meaning it’s easy to verify a factorization but hard to find one, especially for large numbers.

I elaborated on how a polynomial-time algorithm for factorization would imply P = NP, which would revolutionize fields like cryptography, as modern encryption relies on the difficulty of factorization.
